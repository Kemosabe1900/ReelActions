Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your ReelActions account and all associated data at any time.

From inside the app

  1. Open ReelActions and sign in to your account
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted immediately.

By email

If you cannot access the app, send a deletion request from the email address associated with your account to:

support@getreelactions.com

We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile information
  • All saved videos and their AI-generated content
  • All chat history with ReelScout
  • Subscription records and push notification tokens
  • Categories and any data you created in the app

What is retained

We may retain limited records required by law or for fraud prevention (e.g., subscription transaction logs) for the period required by applicable regulations. This data is anonymized where possible and is not accessible from your account.

Questions? See our Privacy Policy.
