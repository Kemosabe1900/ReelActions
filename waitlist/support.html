Common Questions

How do I save a video?

Open TikTok or Instagram, tap Share on any video, and select ReelActions from the share sheet. The video will be processed and added to your library automatically.

How does the AI categorization work?

When you share a video, we transcribe the audio and use AI to summarize the content and assign it to a category in your library. You can change a video's category anytime.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your Apple ID at the top, then tap Subscriptions. Find ReelActions and tap Cancel Subscription. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

You can request account deletion by emailing us at nemsapps@gmail.com. We will delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.

I'm having a technical issue.

Email us at nemsapps@gmail.com with a description of the issue and a screenshot if possible. Please include your device model and iOS version.
